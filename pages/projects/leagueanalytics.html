LEAGUE ANALYTICS

League of Legends Champion Meta Evolution and Synergy Analysis Platform

Project Vision

League Analytics is a data-driven esports intelligence platform designed to uncover champion performance trends, team composition strategies, synergy relationships, and evolving meta dynamics in League of Legends. The project combines advanced analytics, interactive visualizations, and machine learning to transform raw gameplay statistics into actionable strategic insights for players, analysts, coaches, and researchers.
